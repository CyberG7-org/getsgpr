## What a consistency check covers

A consistency check compares information repeated across the application form and supporting records. It is a preparation step, not a separate ICA requirement or an assessment formula.

The aim is to find genuine discrepancies before submission. A discrepancy may be harmless and explainable, but it should not be hidden or "corrected" by altering an official document.

## Begin with identity details

Compare each person's full name, date of birth, passport number and nationality across the application and identification records. Include spouses and children where they form part of the application.

Look for former names, different name order and transliteration differences. If a record uses a previous name, retain the authentic document and gather the official record that connects the two names where applicable.

## Compare the employment timeline

Place the CV, employer letter, payslips and work-pass information side by side. Check the employer name, job title, start date and salary description. The employer letter should contain the details requested in ICA's current checklist.

Job titles sometimes differ between an internal title, an employment contract and a work-pass record. Use accurate wording in the application and explain a material difference instead of selecting whichever title appears more favourable.

## Review education and professional records

Check institution names, qualification titles and completion dates against the certificates and transcripts being uploaded. Make sure a programme that is still in progress is not described as completed.

Documents that are not in English require an official translation accepted by ICA. Keep the source record and translation together so both can be reviewed.

## Check family and civil-status information

Marriage dates, children's details and sponsorship information may appear in several places. Compare the application entries against the relevant civil-status records.

Family circumstances can change between preparing and submitting the application. If a material change occurs, review the form and supporting set again rather than relying on a version prepared earlier.

## Record explanations without rewriting history

Make a short list of differences that remain after the review. For each one, identify the authentic source documents and whether an explanation or additional record is needed.

Do not edit a payslip, certificate, employer letter or civil-status document to make it agree with another file. Correct an error through the organisation that issued the record, or explain the genuine difference accurately.

## Complete the review close to submission

Run the final check after the application form and upload set are complete. Confirm that the files are current, readable and assigned to the correct person. Recheck any record that changed while the application was being prepared.

ICA assesses PR applications holistically and may request additional documents. A consistency review cannot predict the outcome. It supports an accurate application whose repeated facts can be followed across the submitted records.
