## Start with the current ICA checklist

The records needed for a Permanent Residence application depend on the applicant category and the people included. Download the current checklist from ICA before collecting documents. An older saved copy may no longer reflect the current requirements.

For an employed applicant, ICA's checklist includes an employer letter and recent payslips. Other records may apply to the spouse, children or sponsor. Work through the checklist by person rather than placing every document into one unsorted folder.

## Check the employer letter

ICA's checklist asks for a current letter from the employer stating the applicant's occupation, employment date, and monthly basic and gross salary. Check that the letter covers each item before uploading it.

Compare the job title and employment date against the application form, employment pass records, CV and payslips. A genuine variation does not mean a record should be altered. Confirm why the wording differs and provide an accurate explanation where it is relevant.

## Prepare the payslips as one sequence

The checklist calls for six months of payslips for employed applicants. Put them in chronological order and check that each month is present. Make sure the applicant's name, employer and pay period are readable.

Variable pay, bonuses, allowances and deductions can cause the figures to differ from one month to another. The documents should be submitted as issued. If an unusual payment could be confusing when read on its own, keep the supporting employment record available.

## Account for a recent job change

A recent change of employer can leave the application with records from two periods. Use the current employer's documents for the current position and check ICA's latest checklist for the full set required at submission.

Do not describe a role as settled if the supporting records show that it has only just started. Accurate dates make the application easier to review and reduce avoidable inconsistencies.

## Self-employed applicants need a different set

Self-employed applicants and business owners should use the requirements for their own applicant category. ICA may ask for business registration and financial records rather than the same employment documents used by a salaried employee.

Do not substitute a self-written employment letter for a document that the checklist describes differently. Follow the current wording in ICA's checklist and keep foreign-issued tax or business records with any required translation.

## Run a final employment-record check

Before submission, compare the employer letter, payslips, application form, CV and work-pass details. Check names, dates, job titles and salary descriptions. Use readable colour scans and confirm that no page has been cut off.

This review cannot predict ICA's decision. It helps ensure that the employment information is complete, current and internally consistent when submitted.
